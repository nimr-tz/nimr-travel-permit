## Approval Chain (who approves what)

Scientific sections (RCPD, RIRAD) are led by a **Head**; Corporate Services
Directorate (CSD) sections are led by a **Manager** instead — both are the
top of their section and skip straight to their Directorate's Director.

**Scientific-section staff (e.g. `staff.rcpd@nimr.or.tz`, `staff.rirad@nimr.or.tz`)**
→ Head of Section → Director → Director General → HQ HR

**CSD-section staff (e.g. `staff.hq@nimr.or.tz`)**
→ Manager of Section → Director → Director General → HQ HR

**Section Head / Manager travelling (e.g. `head.phps@nimr.or.tz`, `manager.fas@nimr.or.tz`)**
→ Director → Director General → HQ HR

**Directorate Director travelling**
→ Director General → HQ HR

**Standalone-unit staff (e.g. `staff.ict@nimr.or.tz`)**
→ Manager of Unit → Director General → HQ HR

**Standalone-unit Manager travelling (e.g. `manager.ict@nimr.or.tz`)**
→ Director General → HQ HR

**Research Centre Staff (e.g. `staff.mwanza@nimr.or.tz`)**
→ (Supervisor if set) → Centre Manager → HQ HR

**Centre Manager travelling**
→ Director General → HQ HR
